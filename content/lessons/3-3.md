## Brief

This lesson turns the pieces into a routine: running your morning with Claude. The routine is what changes your work, not any clever one-off.

Four steps, one message each:

1. **Triage.** What came in, and what needs me.
2. **Plan.** The three things that matter today.
3. **Clear.** Quick replies drafted, tasks created.
4. **Prep.** What I need for each meeting.

Do it by hand for now. You'll automate it in Module 5.

## Task

Run tomorrow morning with Claude, at the start of a real workday.

1. **Triage.** "Look at everything in my inbox since yesterday evening. Group it: needs a reply from me, needs an action, FYI, ignore. Be decisive."
2. **Plan.** "Look at my calendar today. Combined with the inbox, what are the three things that matter most? Give me a short plan with rough times."
3. **Clear.** "Draft replies to the quick ones and show me all of them in one message." Review and send. Then: "Create tasks in [tool] for the things that need action."
4. **Prep.** "For each meeting today, give me a three-line prep: what it's about, what I need to remember, what I want out of it."
5. **Note the time saved** in the notes box: how long this took versus what you'd normally spend. You'll use that number in Module 6.

## Takeaways

- Triage, plan, clear, prep. One message each, every morning.
- Keep a note of the time saved. It's the reason to automate later.
