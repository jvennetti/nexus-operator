## Brief

You have the pieces now. This lesson puts them together into something you'll actually keep doing: running your morning with Claude in the loop.

The difference between people who "tried connectors" and people whose work changed is **routine.** Not a clever one-off, but the same ten minutes every morning, with Claude doing the parts that used to eat an hour.

A simple shape that works for most people:

1. **Triage.** What came in, what needs me, what can wait.
2. **Plan.** What's on the calendar, what's due, what are the three things that matter today.
3. **Clear.** Draft the replies that are quick. Create the tasks that came out of the inbox. Move what's moveable.
4. **Prep.** For each meeting today, what do I need to remember, and what do I want to get out of it.

Each of those is one message to Claude. The whole thing takes less time than reading your inbox used to.

You'll refine this over the next modules until it runs partly on its own. For now, do it by hand, with Claude, and feel the shape of it.

## Challenge

Run tomorrow morning with Claude. Do this at the start of a real workday.

1. **Triage.** "Look at everything that came into my inbox since yesterday evening. Group it: needs a reply from me, needs an action, FYI only, can be ignored. Be decisive."
2. **Plan.** "Here's what's on my calendar today [or: look at my calendar]. Combined with the inbox, what are the three things that matter most today? Give me a short plan with rough times."
3. **Clear.** "Draft replies to the quick ones. Show me all of them in one message so I can review." Review, edit, send. Then: "Create tasks in [tool] for the things that need action."
4. **Prep.** "For each meeting today, give me a three-line prep: what it's about, what I need to remember, what I want out of it."
5. **Note what it saved.** In the notes box below, write down roughly how long this took and what you would normally have spent. Keep that number. You'll want it in Module 6.

## Takeaways

- The routine matters more than any single clever use. Same ten minutes every morning.
- Triage, plan, clear, prep. Each is one message.
- Keep a rough note of what it saves you. That number becomes the reason to automate it later.
