## Brief

Claude is like a capable colleague who started this morning and knows nothing about you, so what it produces depends on what you tell it. The skill isn't technical, it's directing: knowing what you want, saying it clearly, and pushing back until it's right. This lesson starts your Operator List, the real work you'll practice on for the whole course.

## Task

Make your Operator List.

1. **Write down three tasks from your last week** you'd hand to a capable assistant. Be specific: not "emails" but "reply to the client who asked for a timeline on Thursday."
2. **Open a new chat and paste this,** with your tasks filled in. Read the answer.
```
Here are three tasks from my week that I'd hand to a capable assistant if I had one:

1. [task one]
2. [task two]
3. [task three]

For each one, tell me honestly: could you do it today, partly, or not yet? And for each, what would you need from me (information, files, access) to do it well? Be concrete.
```
3. **Rename the chat "Operator List."** You'll add to it as you go.

## Takeaways

- What Claude produces is shaped by what you tell it. The skill is directing.
- Your Operator List is the real work you'll practice on for the rest of the course.
