## Brief

Build in this order, and don't skip ahead:

1. **Do it by hand** with Claude on real input until the output is right.
2. **Turn it into a skill.**
3. **Connect the input and destination,** testing each with one real item.
4. **Run it end to end twice** while you watch. Fix the skill, not the prompt.
5. **Then add the trigger:** a schedule, or a sentence you say.

Build Version 1 only. Small and working beats big and almost.

When you don't know how to do something, describe the outcome and ask Claude how.

## Task

Build Version 1, end to end.

1. **Manual run.** "Read WORKFLOW.md. Let's do Version 1 manually, step by step, on this real input: [attach or point to it]." Give notes until it's right.
2. **Make the skill.** "Turn that into a skill in /skills, with the output format and an example. Reference WORKFLOW.md."
3. **Connect the ends.** "Pull one real item from [input], process it with the skill, and put the result in [destination]. Show me before you write anything." Then check the destination yourself.
4. **Two full runs** on real input, watching. Fix anything off by editing the skill.
5. **Set the trigger.** Schedule it (drafting, not sending), or write the trigger sentence into WORKFLOW.md: "To run: say '...'"

## Takeaways

- By hand, then skill, then connect, then two test runs, then trigger.
- When you don't know how, describe the outcome and ask Claude how.
