## Brief

Five minutes of orientation saves hours of hunting later. The app is simple, but a few parts of it matter for this course and most people never notice them.

- **Chat and Code.** The two tabs at the top of the sidebar. Chat is where you'll live until Module 4. Code is Claude Code, which can work inside your folders. Find it now so it isn't a mystery later.
- **New chat.** You'll start many. Starting fresh is a skill, not a failure.
- **Your chats.** Recent conversations are listed in the sidebar. Rename the ones you'll come back to.
- **Attaching things.** The plus button in the message box, or drag a file straight in. Documents, spreadsheets, PDFs, screenshots. Claude reads them all.
- **Settings.** Your profile, preferences, and the place to tell Claude about yourself. Connectors live here too. You'll be back in Module 3.

You don't need to understand any of these yet. You need to know where they are.

## Challenge

A scavenger hunt. Find each of these in the desktop app and do the thing.

1. **Find the Code tab.** Just find it. Don't start anything there. Go back to Chat.
2. **Start a new chat** and ask something you've actually been wondering about this week. Anything.
3. **Attach a file two ways.** Drag a document into the message box, and in a second message use the plus button to attach another. Pick a PDF or a spreadsheet for one of them, not just a screenshot. Each time ask: "Summarize this in three sentences, then give me the one thing in it I'm most likely to miss."
4. **Rename the chat.** Find it in the sidebar and give it a name you'd recognize next week.
5. **Open Settings** and find your profile. Where it asks you to tell Claude about yourself, write two sentences: what you do, and what you want help with. Save it.

Once you've done all five, mark the task done.

## Takeaways

- The app has two tabs, Chat and Code. You'll live in Chat until Module 4.
- Attach files with the plus button or by dragging them in. Claude reads documents, spreadsheets, PDFs and screenshots.
- Your chats live in the sidebar. Rename the ones you'll come back to, and tell Claude about yourself once in Settings.
