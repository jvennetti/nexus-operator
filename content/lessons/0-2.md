## Brief

Five minutes of orientation saves hours of hunting later. This video is a room-by-room tour of the desktop app: the two tabs, the chat box, the plus menu, and where Settings live.

## Challenge

Find your way around.

1. Click the **Code** tab at the top of the sidebar. Look at how the left panel changes. Click back to **Chat**.
2. Click **New chat** and ask something you've actually been wondering about this week.
3. Click the **+** button in the chat box and read the whole menu once. Then attach a file (drag it in, or use the + button) and paste:
```
Summarize this in three sentences, then tell me the one thing in it I'm most likely to miss.
```
4. In the sidebar, find that chat and rename it to something you'd recognize next week.
5. Click your name (bottom left) → **Settings** → **Profile**. Where it asks about you, write two sentences: what you do, and what you want help with. Save.

Mark this done when you've done all five.

## Takeaways

- Two tabs. Chat is where you'll live until Module 4. Code can work inside your folders.
- The + button in the chat box is where features hide: files, folders, connectors, skills.
- Tell Claude about yourself once, in Settings → Profile. It uses it in every chat.
- Rename the chats you'll come back to.
