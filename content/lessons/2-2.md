## Brief

Claude works best when you give it the real material, not a tidy summary of it. This video shows someone handing over raw, messy data and checking the result against what he already knows. You're going to do the same with your own work.

## Challenge

Give Claude the real thing.

1. Pick something you've already done: a reply you sent, a summary you wrote, a spreadsheet you worked through.
2. Gather the raw material behind it: the whole email thread, the original file, the messy notes. Don't tidy it.
3. Open a new chat, attach or paste it all, and write one paragraph: what this is, what you want, and what you care about.
4. Compare what Claude made with what you actually produced. Tell it one thing it missed and see if it fixes it.
5. Write down the thing it missed as "Always tell Claude: ..." You'll use that line again.

Mark this done when Claude's version is as good as yours, or you know exactly why it isn't.

## Takeaways

- Paste the whole thread. Attach the original file. Claude can read it all, so you don't need to summarize first.
- Test Claude on work you've already done, so you can tell what it got right.
- When it misses something, that's a piece of context you give it next time.
