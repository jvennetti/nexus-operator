## Brief

So far you've been getting text back. Claude can also build things: documents, slide decks, spreadsheets, designs, web pages, interactive tools. In chat, right now, no extra software.

You don't need to learn a feature for each one. You describe what you want and Claude produces it. "Make this a one-page PDF." "Turn these notes into a slide deck for Monday." "Build me a simple page that tracks my weekly habits." "Design a landing page for this offer." It just does it, and you can keep giving notes until it's right.

What matters is the same thing that always matters: **context and clarity.** The difference between a generic design and one that looks like yours is whether you told Claude what yours looks like. Attach a screenshot of your current site. Paste your brand colors. Show an example you love. Say who it's for and what they should do when they see it.

A good frame for describing anything you want built: **what it is, who it's for, what it needs to contain, and how it should feel.** "A one-page proposal for a landscaping client, three sections (what we'll do, timeline, price), clean and confident, like this example."

You'll be surprised how often "just describe it" is the whole answer.

## Challenge

Build one thing you'll actually use this week.

1. **Choose something you need.** A document, a one-pager, a slide deck, a simple tracker, a design. If stuck: a one-page summary of a project you're working on, for someone who hasn't been following it.
2. **Describe it with the frame:** what it is, who it's for, what it needs to contain, how it should feel. Attach anything that shows your style: a screenshot, a past doc, a logo.
3. **Let Claude build it.** Then give it at least three rounds of notes, the way you learned in 2.3. Structure first, then wording, then look.
4. **Export or save the result** in whatever form you need. Put it where you'd actually use it.
5. **Use it.** Send it, present it, open it tomorrow.

## Takeaways

- Claude builds documents, decks, spreadsheets, designs and simple tools directly in chat. Describe it, don't look for a button.
- Frame any build as: what it is, who it's for, what it contains, how it feels.
- Style comes from context. Show Claude what yours looks like and it will match it.
