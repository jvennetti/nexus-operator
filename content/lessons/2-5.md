## Brief

Claude can build things, not just write text: documents, slide decks, spreadsheets, designs, simple pages and tools, right in chat. There's no feature to learn. Describe what you want and give notes until it's right.

Describe any build in four parts: **what it is, who it's for, what it contains, how it should feel.** For example: "A one-page proposal for a landscaping client, three sections (scope, timeline, price), clean and confident, like this example."

Style comes from context. Attach a screenshot, your brand colors, or an example you love.

## Task

Build one thing you'll use this week.

1. **Choose something you need:** a one-pager, a slide deck, a tracker, a design. If stuck, a one-page summary of a project for someone who hasn't been following it.
2. **Describe it in the four parts** and attach anything that shows your style.
3. **Give at least three rounds of notes:** structure first, then wording, then look.
4. **Save it and use it.** Send it, present it, or open it tomorrow.

## Takeaways

- Describe the thing you want built. Don't look for a button.
- What it is, who it's for, what it contains, how it feels. Style comes from what you show it.
