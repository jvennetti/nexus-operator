## Brief

Now connect the tools where work gets done: your task manager, docs, drive or team chat. Connect the one or two you live in, not everything.

The pattern is always the same. Information sits in one tool, needs to be in another, and you used to carry it across by hand. Now you say a sentence:

- "Create tasks in ClickUp for everything we promised in this thread."
- "Find our onboarding doc in Drive and tell me what it says about week one."
- "Draft a status update from my open tasks."

Ask Claude to show you what it will create before it creates it.

## Task

Connect your main work tool and carry something across.

1. **Connect one or two work tools** in Settings → Connectors.
2. **Carry something across.** Take an email thread or meeting notes and say: "Create tasks in [tool] for everything that needs doing here. Assign to me, due this week, and show me the list before you create them." Review, then approve.
3. **Pull something out.** "Summarize everything I have open in [tool], grouped by project, most urgent first."
4. **Check the tool itself.** Confirm the tasks are there and look right. Fix anything off by telling Claude.

## Takeaways

- Connect the tools you live in. The core move is carrying information between them with a sentence.
- Have Claude show you what it will create, then approve.
