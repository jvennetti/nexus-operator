## Brief

A scheduled task is a prompt that runs by itself on Claude's servers, with your connectors, at a time you set. This video builds a morning email report from scratch, with no code. You'll build yours from the morning brief you already wrote.

## Challenge

Make your morning brief run by itself.

1. Open your morning brief prompt from Module 3. Add what to ignore (newsletters, receipts, notifications) and this rule:
```
Don't reply to anyone, don't archive anything, don't mark anything read.
```
2. In the sidebar, find **Scheduled** → **New task**. Name it, paste your prompt, and set it for a few minutes past the hour tomorrow morning.
3. In the task's settings, check Gmail and Calendar are connected to it, and turn on email notifications so the report lands in your inbox.
4. Press **Run now**. Read the report: what it skipped, what it flagged.
5. Tomorrow, open the run and read it. Don't trust a green tick. Read the output.

Mark this done after two runs you've actually read. [Scheduled tasks need a paid plan.]

## Takeaways

- A scheduled task is a prompt, a time and your connectors. That's the whole thing.
- Tell it what to ignore and what not to touch. Reading is safe. Writing is yours.
- Run it by hand twice before you trust the schedule.
- Warning: if you set a send tool to "always allow," Claude can send to anyone. Only your instructions stop it.
