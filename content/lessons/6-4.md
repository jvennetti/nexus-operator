## Brief

Habits worth picking up. Take the ones that fit.

- **Speak, don't type.** Dictation makes briefs longer and better, because you say the context you'd never type.
- **Keep a prompts file.** When a prompt works, save it. Next time, paste and adjust.
- **Ask what it needs.** "What would you need from me to do this really well?"
- **Ask it to be harsh.** "Tell me the three biggest weaknesses in this plan." Claude defaults to agreeable.
- **When in doubt, start over.** Fresh chat, one clean message, everything it needs.
- **Tell it you want it done.** When it hedges or gives you the safe version: "I want you to do it. Fully. Here's what you need."

## Takeaways

- Speak your briefs, save prompts that work, ask for harsh feedback.
- When stuck, start over with one clean message. When Claude hedges, tell it you want it done.
