## Brief

Before you build anything, pick the right thing. This video shows why most automation copies steps that should have been deleted, with a real example that went from seven steps to four. You'll map one piece of your own work and cut it down.

## Challenge

Pick your workflow and cut it.

1. List three things you do every week by hand that take an hour or more.
2. Pick the one that annoys you most. In the **Code** tab, on nexus-workspace, paste:
```
Create WORKFLOW.md and interview me to fill it in: the input (what starts it), the steps, the output, and where the output goes.
```
3. Next to each step, write Keep, Cut or Change. The test: does a person act on what this step produces? If not, cut it.
4. Mark any step where money, legal or your reputation is at stake. Those stay yours: Claude drafts, you decide.
5. Add a line at the bottom: "Version 1 does: ..." covering only the Keep steps.

Mark this done when WORKFLOW.md has a Version 1 line.

## Takeaways

- Map it before you automate it: input, steps, output, destination.
- Cut steps before you automate them. Half the work in most workflows is tidying for someone who doesn't need it.
- Version 1 is the smallest thing that's useful. Build that first.
