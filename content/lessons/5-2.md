## Brief

Long chats and sessions get worse because Claude re-reads the whole conversation every time, and as it grows, early instructions get crowded out; that limit is the **context window**. The signs are repeating itself, forgetting a rule, getting vaguer. The fix: keep standing context in files (Projects, `CLAUDE.md`, skills), start fresh often, and when a long conversation isn't finished, carry a short summary into a new one.

## Task

Rescue a long conversation and feel the difference.

1. **Find a long chat or session** from earlier in the course.
2. **Ask for the handoff** at the end of it:
```
Summarize where we are: what we've decided, what's done, what's left, and any rules or preferences I've given you in this conversation. Keep it under 15 lines.
```
3. **Start fresh.** New chat or session, paste the summary, and give it the next step of the task. Notice it's faster and remembers the rules.
4. **Move the standing stuff out.** Anything in the summary that's a permanent rule belongs in Project instructions or `CLAUDE.md`. Move it now.

## Takeaways

- Long conversations crowd out early instructions. Start fresh often.
- Standing context belongs in files. Unfinished work moves to a new chat as a short summary.
