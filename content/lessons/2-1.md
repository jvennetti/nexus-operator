## Brief

This video shows you the three things a good prompt needs: context, examples, and the shape of the output you want. You'll use all three in the challenge.

## Challenge

Take a bad prompt and make it great with the rules you just learned.

1. Open a new chat and paste this exactly. Read what Claude says. Don't fix it yet.
```
Write an email to a client about the delay.
```
2. Open another new chat. Rewrite the same prompt with what you learned:
   1. the goal
   2. why it matters
   3. who you are and who the email is for
   4. the shape of the output (under 120 words, a subject line, one clear ask at the end)
3. Use a real example from your own work if you have one.

Mark this done when the second email is one you'd actually send.

## Takeaways

- Claude starts from zero every time. What you want, why, and who you are need saying to get great results.
- Add examples of what you like for better results.
- List the details you want: length, format, how it should end.
- Tell Claude what your goal is.
