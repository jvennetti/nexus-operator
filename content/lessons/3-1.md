## Brief

We're going to learn about connectors. Connectors let Claude use your everyday tools, like Gmail, Google Calendar or ClickUp. You're going to see how much time comes back when Claude handles the small everyday tasks. Enjoy!

## Challenge

Connect Claude to your Gmail.

1. Tell Claude you want to connect your Gmail and Google Calendar. Follow what it says, and say yes when it asks for permission.
2. In a new chat, paste:
```
I haven't looked at my inbox today. Read the last 24 hours and tell me what needs a reply today.
```
3. Pick one email that needs a reply and paste this, filled in. Then open Gmail and find it in Drafts.
```
Draft a reply to [sender] offering two times I'm free this week, based on my calendar. Do not send it.
```
4. Read the draft and make any changes before sending it off. Eventually you'll learn how to get emails you can send without editing. This is a great start.

Mark this done when a reply Claude drafted has actually gone out.

## Takeaways

- Claude can connect to your daily tools.
- You can have Claude draft and even send emails for you. [Be careful: keep sending on "needs approval" so nothing goes out without you knowing.]
- Claude writes in your voice because it has read your past emails.
