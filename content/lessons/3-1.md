## Brief

Until now, Claude only knew what you pasted in. Connectors change that. A connector gives Claude permission to read from, and act in, a tool you already use. Connect Gmail and Claude can read your inbox, find the thread you half-remember, and draft the reply. Connect your calendar and it can see your week.

Where they live: open Settings in the Claude app and look for **Connectors**. You'll see a list of apps. Connecting one means signing in to that app and approving access. You can disconnect any of them at any time.

Two things to understand before you connect anything:

- **You choose the scope.** Claude only sees what the connector allows, and only when it's relevant to what you asked. It doesn't wander through your email on its own.
- **Reading is easy, acting asks.** Claude will read freely. When it's about to do something with consequences (send, delete, move), it checks with you first. You stay the one who presses send.

Once connected, you don't need any special commands. You just talk about your email and calendar the way you'd talk to an assistant who has access to them. "What did Sarah say about the budget?" "What's on Thursday?" "Draft a reply to the latest from the accountant." Claude figures out the rest.

## Task

Connect two tools and get a real morning briefing.

1. **Connect Gmail and Google Calendar** (or whatever email and calendar you actually use) in Settings → Connectors. Approve the access.
2. **Ask for the briefing.** New chat:
```
Look at my inbox from the last two days and my calendar for the rest of this week. Give me: the five emails that most need a reply from me and why, anything that looks urgent, and a short plan for my week based on what's on the calendar. Keep it tight.
```
3. **Have it find something.** Think of an email you'd normally dig for: "Find the message where [person] mentioned [thing] and tell me what they asked for."
4. **Draft one reply.** "Draft a reply to [that email]. Keep it short, in my usual tone." Review it the way you learned in Module 2. Send it yourself, or let Claude send it if you're comfortable.
5. **Put something on the calendar.** "Block 45 minutes tomorrow morning for [a task from your Operator List]."

## Takeaways

- Connectors live in Settings. Connecting an app means Claude can read from it and act in it when relevant.
- You pick the scope and you can disconnect at any time. Claude asks before doing anything with consequences.
- No special commands. Talk about your inbox and calendar the way you'd talk to an assistant who has them open.
