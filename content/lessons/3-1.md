## Brief

Connectors let Claude read from and act in the tools you already use, starting with email and calendar. You turn them on in Settings → Connectors; Claude reads freely but asks before anything with consequences, like sending or deleting. After that there are no special commands: talk about your inbox the way you would to an assistant who has it open.

## Task

Connect two tools and get a real morning briefing.

1. **Connect your email and calendar** in Settings → Connectors.
2. **Ask for the briefing.** New chat:
```
Look at my inbox from the last two days and my calendar for the rest of this week. Give me: the five emails that most need a reply from me and why, anything that looks urgent, and a short plan for my week based on what's on the calendar. Keep it tight.
```
3. **Have it find something** you'd normally dig for: "Find the message where [person] mentioned [thing] and tell me what they asked for."
4. **Draft one reply.** "Draft a reply to [that email]. Short, in my usual tone." Review it, then send it.
5. **Put something on the calendar.** "Block 45 minutes tomorrow morning for [a task from your Operator List]."

## Takeaways

- Connectors live in Settings. Claude asks before doing anything with consequences.
- No special commands. Talk to it like an assistant who has your inbox open.
