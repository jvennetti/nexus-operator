## Brief

`CLAUDE.md` is a file at the top of your folder that Claude Code reads automatically at the start of every session. It holds your rules and preferences (how to write, where things go, what never to do), while other files hold information. When you catch yourself saying something twice, say "add that to CLAUDE.md."

## Task

Write your instructions file, then let it grow.

1. **Ask Claude to draft it.** In your workspace:
```
Create a CLAUDE.md for this workspace. Base it on ABOUT-ME.md and what you know about how I work. Cover: how I want things written, where finished work goes, what to always do before creating or changing files, what to never do, and any conventions for file names. Keep it short and direct. Show it to me before saving.
```
2. **Edit it.** Cut what isn't true. Add the thing you keep saying.
3. **Test one rule.** Give Claude a task that should trigger a rule in the file. Confirm it follows the rule without a reminder.
4. **Teach it a rule in the moment.** Next time you correct Claude, follow up with: "Add that to CLAUDE.md so I don't have to say it again." Check that it did.

## Takeaways

- `CLAUDE.md` is read at the start of every session. Rules go there; information goes in other files.
- Say something twice, then say "add that to CLAUDE.md."
