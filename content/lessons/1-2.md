## Brief

Working with Claude is managing. This video is about treating AI like a brilliant new hire: it knows a lot about everything and nothing about your business. You're going to brief it like a manager and review the draft like one.

## Challenge

Get one real task done, start to finish.

1. Pick the easiest task from your list, the one Claude said it could do today.
2. Before you write anything, note three things a new hire on day one couldn't know about this task. Put them in your message.
3. Open a new chat and brief it: the outcome you want, who it's for, and what a good result looks like. Attach or paste anything relevant.
4. Read the draft like a manager. Send at least two rounds of notes. Be blunt: "too long," "wrong tone," "you missed the deadline."
5. If Claude hedges or says it can't, paste this and give it what it needs:
```
I understand. I still want you to do it. Here's what you need:
```
6. Use the result for real. Send the email, file the document, use the plan.

Mark this done when the real thing is done.

## Takeaways

- Brief Claude like you'd brief a person: outcome, audience, context, what good looks like.
- The first draft is a starting point. Notes and a second round are normal.
- When Claude hedges or says it can't, tell it plainly you want it done. It almost always can.
