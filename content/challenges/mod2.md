## Scenario

**The Thing You've Been Putting Off.** There is something on your list that keeps sliding to next week. It's rarely because the work is hard. It's because starting it is hard, and nothing forces the start. This mission is the force.

You now know how to brief Claude properly, feed it the real material, push for a better draft, keep your context in a project, and build small things. You'll use all of that on one real piece of work, inside your Nexus Academy project, and you'll finish it today. Along the way you'll give Claude a short profile of you, so every chat in the project starts knowing who it's talking to.

## Steps

1. Pick the task. The one you keep moving. Open a new chat inside your Nexus Academy project.
2. Before you brief the task, give Claude you. Paste this, answer its questions honestly, and put the result into the project's instructions:
```
Interview me, one question at a time, then write a short profile for my project instructions: what I do, who I work with, how I like things written, three things you should always do when working with me, and two things you should never do.
```
   Tip: specific beats flattering. "I write short emails and hate exclamation marks" is worth more than "I value clear communication." While you're there, add your My Tasks file to the project's files.
3. Now brief the task the way Module 2 taught you: the goal, why it matters, who it's for, and what the finished thing looks like. End the message with "Before you start, ask me what you need to know," and answer what it asks. If you're not sure what finished looks like, say so, and ask Claude for two versions to choose between.
4. Read the draft as the person who will receive it, not as the person who wrote the brief. Give three specific notes in one message. Then two more. Then use it for real: send it, file it, present it. The task is only done when the real-world thing is done.
5. One more thing, because it's the habit that pays off most: build a small tool for this task or the next one. A checklist, a tracker, a one-page summary. Ask for it as an artifact and open it again tomorrow.

## Deliverable

You're done when the thing you were putting off is finished and used, your profile is in the project instructions, and you have one built thing you've opened twice.

## Extension

Inside the project, paste: "Based on my profile and my tasks, what are three things I could be asking you for that I haven't thought of?" Try one of them this week.
