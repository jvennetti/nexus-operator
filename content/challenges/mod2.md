## Scenario

Module 2 gave you the language: goal, context, format, example. Iteration. Projects. Building things. This challenge locks all of it in by building the one piece of context you'll reuse more than any other: a profile of you.

Every Project you create from now on starts with it. Every workspace in Claude Code will hold a copy. It's the answer to "who am I talking to?" that Claude otherwise has to guess.

## Steps

1. **Let Claude interview you.** New chat, paste this:
```
I'm going to give you information about myself. Your job is to help me write an profile: a document I'll paste into every Claude Project I create. It should cover: my name and what I do, the kinds of tasks I most often need help with, how I like things written (tone, length, formality), three things you should always do when working with me, and two things you should never do. Interview me one question at a time until you have what you need. Then write the document.
```
   Answer honestly. Specific beats flattering.
2. **Edit until it sounds like you.** Cut anything generic. If a line could describe anyone, rewrite it so it only describes you. Save the final version somewhere permanent.
3. **Build the Project.** Create a Project called **Nexus Academy**. Paste the profile into its instructions. Add your task list document from Module 1 to its knowledge.
4. **Produce one real deliverable inside it.** Pick the top item from your task list. Inside Nexus Academy, with no re-explaining, brief Claude on it, iterate at least three rounds, and produce the real output. Use it.
5. **Make one thing.** Still inside the Project, build something you'll use this week: a one-page summary, a tracker, a deck, a design. Describe it with the frame from Lesson 2.5.

## Deliverable

- Your profile, edited and saved.
- A Project called Nexus Academy with the profile as instructions and your task list in its knowledge.
- One real deliverable produced inside the Project and used in the real world.
- One built thing (document, page, tracker, deck) that you'll open again this week.

## Extension

Ask Claude, inside Nexus Academy: "Based on my profile and task list, what are three things I could be asking you for that I haven't thought of?" Try one.
