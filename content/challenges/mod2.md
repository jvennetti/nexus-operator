## Scenario

You can brief Claude, feed it the real thing, push back, keep context in a project, and build things. This mission puts all of it to work on one real piece of your own work, inside your Nexus Academy project.

## Steps

1. In a new chat, paste this and answer its questions:
```
Interview me, one question at a time, and then write a short profile I can paste into my project instructions: what I do, who I work with, how I like things written, three things you should always do when working with me, and two things you should never do.
```
2. Edit the profile until it sounds like you. Paste it into the Nexus Academy project instructions. Add your My Tasks document to the project's files.
3. Inside the project, pick one task from your list and get it done for real. Brief it properly, have Claude ask you questions first, give at least two rounds of notes, and use the result.
4. Build one thing you'll open again this week (a tracker, a one-page summary, a checklist). Use it today.

## Deliverable

- Your profile in the project instructions.
- One real task done inside the project and used in the real world.
- One built thing you've already opened twice.

## Extension

Inside the project, paste: "Based on my profile and my tasks, what are three things I could be asking you for that I haven't thought of?" Try one.
