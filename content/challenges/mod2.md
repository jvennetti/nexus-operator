## Scenario

**The Thing You've Been Putting Off.** Something on your list keeps slipping to next week. Not because it's hard, usually. Because starting it is. This mission gets it finished, properly, inside your Nexus Academy project.

## Steps

1. Pick it. The task you keep moving. Open a chat inside Nexus Academy.
2. Before you brief Claude, give it who you are. Paste this and answer its questions:
```
Interview me, one question at a time, then write a short profile for my project instructions: what I do, who I work with, how I like things written, three things you should always do when working with me, and two things you should never do.
```
   Put the result in the project instructions. Add your My Tasks file to the project's files.
3. Now brief the task. Goal, why it matters, who it's for, what the finished thing looks like. End with: "Before you start, ask me what you need to know."
   Tip: if you're not sure what "finished" looks like, say that. Ask Claude for two options and pick one.
4. Read the draft the way the person receiving it would. Give three specific notes. Then two more. Use the result for real.
5. One more thing: build a small tool for this task or the next one (a checklist, a tracker, a one-page summary). Open it tomorrow.

## Deliverable

- The thing you've been putting off, done and used.
- Your profile in the project instructions, your My Tasks file in the project.
- One built thing you've opened twice.

## Extension

Inside the project, paste: "Based on my profile and my tasks, what are three things I could be asking you for that I haven't thought of?" Try one.
