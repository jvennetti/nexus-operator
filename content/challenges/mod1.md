## Scenario

You've made a short list of work you'd hand off, and you've handed one piece off for real. This mission turns the list into the document you'll build the rest of the school against.

## Steps

1. Open your "My tasks" chat. Add two more tasks from your week until you have five. Each one specific enough that a stranger would know what it is.
2. Paste:
```
For each of my five tasks, tell me what you'd need to know, see, or have access to in order to do it well. Keep it to one line per task.
```
3. Paste:
```
Turn this into a clean one-page document called My Tasks: the five tasks, what you'd need for each, and which one costs me the most time per week.
```
4. Save the document somewhere you'll find it. You'll put it in your project in Module 2.

## Deliverable

- A one-page My Tasks document, saved.

## Note

No job or business that makes this kind of work? Use your life. Meals, budget, a club, a hobby, job hunting. It all counts.
