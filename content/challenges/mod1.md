## Scenario

**The Handoff.** There's work on your plate that someone else could do, if that someone existed. Now it does. The question is which work, and what it would take to hand it over well.

## Steps

1. Open your "My tasks" chat. You have three tasks there. Look at your calendar and your sent mail from last week and add two more. Five in total, each one specific enough that a stranger would know what it is.
2. For each task, ask yourself: do I know what "done" looks like? If you can't say it in a sentence, Claude can't either. Write that sentence next to the task.
3. Paste:
```
For each of my five tasks, tell me what you'd need to know, see, or have access to in order to do it well. One line per task. Then tell me which one you'd start with and why.
```
   Tip: if Claude's answer surprises you, that's the useful part. Note what it asked for that you hadn't thought of.
4. Paste:
```
Turn this into a one-page document called My Tasks: the five tasks, what "done" looks like for each, what you'd need, and which one costs me the most time per week.
```
5. Save it somewhere permanent. It goes into your project in Module 2.

## Deliverable

- My Tasks: one page, five tasks, saved.

## Note

Not everyone's week runs on a job. Meals, a budget, a club, a hobby, a job hunt: all of it is work with inputs and outputs, and all of it counts.
