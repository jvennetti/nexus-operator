## Scenario

You've done two things so far: made a list of work you'd hand off, and handed one piece of it off for real. This challenge turns that list into the document you'll build the rest of the course against.

Most people never write this down. They have a vague sense that "AI could probably help with some stuff" and never get more specific than that. You're going to get specific.

## Steps

1. **Expand the list to five.** Open your "Operator List" chat. Add two more tasks from your week until you have five, each with its automate / augment / keep tag. Each one specific enough that a stranger would know what it is.
2. **Add the column that matters.** For each task, ask Claude: "What would you need to know, see, or have access to in order to do this well?" Write the answer next to each task. Files, context, tools, examples. This column is the course syllabus, personalized to you.
3. **Rank them.** Order the five by how much time they cost you per week. The top one is a candidate for your Module 6 system. Note it.
4. **Make it a document.** Ask Claude: "Turn this into a clean one-page document called Operator List, with the five tasks, their tags, what you'd need for each, and the time ranking." Save it somewhere you'll find it. You'll put it in a Project in Module 2 and a workspace in Module 4.

## Deliverable

- A one-page Operator List: five specific tasks, each tagged, what Claude would need for each, ranked by weekly time cost.
- Saved somewhere permanent. You'll use it in Modules 2, 4 and 6.

## Note

If you don't have a job or business that generates this kind of work, use your life. Planning meals, managing a household budget, organizing a club, keeping up with a hobby, job hunting. All of it is pattern work. All of it counts.
