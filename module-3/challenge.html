The Challenge
The Scenario

You've connected your tools and run a morning with Claude. This challenge is a full working day, on real work, with Claude in every tool you use. Not a demo with fake data. Tomorrow.

The goal isn't to prove Claude can do your job. It's to find out, concretely, which parts of your day it takes off your plate when it's actually wired in, and how much time that is. You'll want that number in Module 6.

Before you build

Make sure these are connected before you start the day: your email, your calendar, and the one work tool you use most. If something isn't connected yet, connect it the night before.

Your Challenge
  1. 1
    Morning: the routine. Run the four-step morning from Lesson 3.3: triage, plan, clear, prep. Time it.
  2. 2
    Midday: carry things across. At least twice during the day, when information arrives in one tool and needs to be somewhere else, say the sentence instead of doing it by hand. "Create tasks from this." "Add this to the doc." "Put that on the calendar." Each time, review before approving.
  3. 3
    Afternoon: draft everything. Every reply you'd normally write from scratch, have Claude draft first. Review, edit, send. Count how many.
  4. 4
    End of day: the update. "Look at what I did today across my tools and draft a short end-of-day update I could send to my team (or to myself)." Read it. Is it accurate? What did it miss?
  5. 5
    Write the tally. In a new chat: how long the morning routine took versus normal, how many replies Claude drafted, how many things got carried across, and roughly how much time the day saved. Keep this. Add it to your Operator List document.
Your Deliverable
  • One real workday run with Claude connected to your email, calendar and main work tool.
  • At least two "carry it across" moves and a set of drafted replies, all reviewed and used.
  • A short written tally of what the day saved, added to your Operator List document.

If your day doesn't generate much email or tasks, run this on a day you're organizing something personal: a trip, a move, an event, a job search. Same tools, same moves.
