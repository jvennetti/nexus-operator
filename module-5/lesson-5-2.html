Context Management

Long chats get worse. Claude starts ignoring instructions and forgetting facts, because its working memory fills up. This video explains why, the four warning signs, and the handoff that fixes it.

Notes
Key Takeaways
Long chats degrade. The start and the end get remembered. The middle gets lost.
Hand off with a summary and start fresh. It takes seconds, and the new chat is sharper.
Permanent instructions belong in a project or CLAUDE.md, not repeated in chat.
Your Challenge

Rescue a tired chat.

  1. 1
    Open an old long chat. Find one of the four signs: ignored instructions, contradictions, forgotten facts, or a notice that it compacted the conversation.
  2. 2
    Paste:
    Summarize this for a fresh chat, under 15 lines: what we've covered, decisions made, where we left off, and the exact next step.
  3. 3
    Start a new chat, paste the summary, and give it the next step. Compare the quality.
  4. 4
    Move anything permanent you were repeating into your project instructions or CLAUDE.md.
  5. 5
    Next time you attach a big file, give only the part that matters.

Mark this done when the fresh chat picks up where the old one left off.
