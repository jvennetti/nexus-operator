How to Win With AI in 2026

Hormozi's take is blunter than most: AI will never be worse than it is right now, and if you're not treating this as your top priority, you're already behind. This video reframes AI from a productivity tool into a competitive position, and makes the case for why being early matters so much more than being perfect.

The key conceptual shift he introduces: stop thinking about prompting, start thinking about context systems. The people winning with AI aren't the ones writing clever prompts — they're the ones who've built the context infrastructure that makes every prompt more powerful.
