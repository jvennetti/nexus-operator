Anatomy of a Good Prompt

A prompt is a brief. Most people write it like a text to a friend, then wonder why the answer is generic.

A good prompt has four parts: the goal, the context Claude can't know, the format you want, and an example of what good looks like.

  1. Open a new chat in Claude and paste this:
    Write an email to my client about the delay.
  2. Open another new chat. Ask for the same email, this time with a goal, context, format and example.
  3. Read both answers. Which one could you actually send?
Key Takeaways
Could a smart stranger act on your prompt? If not, neither can Claude.
One real example beats paragraphs of description.
